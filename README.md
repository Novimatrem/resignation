> **Resignation** is the acceptance of something undesirable but inevitable.  
> Example: *"A shrug of resignation."*

<br>

> **Loss #1** is the ability to have audio, anymore.  
> *Despite years of work on many multiple scripts to attempt to limit the audio of my systems to reasonable degrees, all of which I believed to be successful until very recently, the ability for me to have audio is no longer something that is possible. This means that any events that I would have to attend on a regular basis, such as groups, showers, and even the acts of waking up and going to sleep, will now be forced to return to their previous un-notified and completely random forms, because I cannot be alerted to when they begin.*<br><br>
> **In the code:** This loss is reflected by the script setting the volume to 0% each loop.

<br>

> **Loss #2** is the ability to have communicate, anymore.  
> *Despite many (I had believed) learning experiences, many blog posts in which I discuss this openly, and incredible limits to the specific platforms that I communicate via, in order to allow the most flexibility in regards to character count, response time, and attached content, it seems that communicating is not something that I will be able to continue. I seem to be automatically taken at my worse during communications, and this has held especially true lately. Despite the incredible amount of verbosity granted by me lately, and the insistence on the levels of communication that I will accept, including having no issues with very large delays during the communication process as long as everything clearly gets across as intended, it has not ended in a way that in fact has taken advantage of any benefits afforded to it. The process of communication remains as volatile as ever, despite 100% of the work done to attempt to alleviate that. As such, this means that I will have to give up on communicating in any form that is intended to receive any reply or response, in any way.*<br><br>
> **In the code:** This loss is reflected by the script killing places where communication happens, for example Steam, each loop, and in cases where that would take too much functionality away is instead handled by the Tampermonkey scripts located on the following page: [https://greasyfork.org/en/users/1077898-novimatrem-zoey-globe](https://greasyfork.org/en/users/1077898-novimatrem-zoey-globe)
